# Security policy

Hatifect is currently developed from the `develop` branch. Security fixes are
provided only for the current repository state unless a release explicitly
states otherwise. No support, response time, fix, or continued availability is
guaranteed.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue for a suspected vulnerability. Use GitHub's private
vulnerability reporting from the repository's **Security** tab, or another
private contact method designated by `ihatectf` in the repository.

Include:

- the affected component and revision;
- reproduction steps or a minimal proof of concept;
- the expected impact;
- relevant environment details; and
- any suggested mitigation, if known.

Remove unrelated personal data, credentials, save files, and third-party
proprietary material. A report should contain only the portions of Hatifect
reasonably necessary to investigate the vulnerability.

## Coordinated disclosure

Public disclosure is permitted on the earliest of:

- `ihatectf` publicly releasing a fix;
- `ihatectf` providing written authorization; or
- 90 days after `ihatectf` receives a sufficiently detailed private report
  that reasonably enables investigation.

Before that point, do not disclose the vulnerability or exploit in a public
issue, pull request, repository, advisory, post, or other publication. When
disclosure becomes permitted, limit it to what is reasonably necessary to
explain and verify the vulnerability. Nothing in this policy restricts a
disclosure required by applicable law, but give advance notice when legally
permitted.

## License scope

Security research does not grant permission beyond Section 9 of
[LICENSE](LICENSE). In particular, it does not authorize unrelated access,
distribution, hosted use, service provision, or publication of excessive
portions of Hatifect.
